# Backlog — ideas for quality of life, fun and replayability

Suggested 2026-11-02. ✅ = done, 🔨 = in progress.

## Quality of life
- ✅ **Loot comparison arrows**: ▲/▼ on Damage, Armor, HP (and Crit, Dodge) vs what you're wearing, on drops, picks, the shop and the backpack.
- **"New" dots**: a dot on Spoils / Aspects / Extras when something new lands there (relics, curses, blessings).
- **Fight speed + skip**: a speed toggle in the combat bar, and "skip to result" once a fight is clearly won.
- **Bulk sell/scrap**: one button to clear all Commons (or everything below a rarity) from the bag.
- ✅ **Run history**: the last 10 runs: class, floor, killer, final build.

## Fun
- **Build moments**: small fanfares when a combo clicks (first reaction, a Smite, a 10× hit, a full Legend set).
- **Relic choice**: sometimes offer a pick of two relics instead of a random one, so players can steer their build.
- **Nemesis**: whatever killed your last run sometimes returns, stronger, with a bounty on it.
- **Legendary lore**: the first time you find each Legendary, a one-line lore popup.

## Replayability
- ✅ **Daily Dungeon**: the same seed (and class) for everyone each day, one attempt, with its own leaderboard.
- **Achievements with unlocks**: e.g. "beat a Champion on floor 1", "win without potions", "reach floor 20 cursed"; reward cosmetic titles or starting-kit options.
- **Class mastery**: cross-run XP per class that unlocks alternate starting kits (e.g. a two-handed Warrior).
- **Heat levels**: optional campaign modifiers chosen before a run (+25% enemy HP, no Campfires…) for more Renown.
- **Abyss weekly modifier**: one rotating twist per week ("all Champions are Crimson", "chests are Mimics").
